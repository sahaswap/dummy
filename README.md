137237_ALERT2363759_Escalation Narrative

This case is being escalated to Community Federal Savings Bank ("CFSB," the "Bank") 
to report 56 transactions totaling $3,425,039.74 conducted by subject Sundex Trading 
Limited through Airwallex accounts.

1. Subject: Sundex Trading Limited

2. Type of Suspicious Activity: Suspicion concerning the source of funds, suspicious 
EFT/wire transfers, transaction(s) with no apparent economic, business, or lawful 
purpose, transaction(s) inconsistent with subject's stated industry type, other: 
higher dollar, higher volume

3. Total Suspicious Dollar Amount: $3,425,039.74

4. Date Range of Suspicious Activity: 03/03/2026 through 06/26/2026

5. Associated Program Partners: Airwallex

6. Financial Institutions and Accounts Involved: CFSB, MSP8485508762

Based on CFSB's records, Sundex Trading Limited is an indirect customer of CFSB and 
is a client of the Airwallex program. Airwallex is a payments company offering 
multi-currency business accounts, payment acceptance, money transfers, and virtual 
corporate cards, enabling businesses to operate across jurisdictions. Airwallex 
maintains accounts with CFSB to facilitate money remittances and process card 
payments via the processor i2C.

Sundex Trading Limited's stated industry type on file is Wholesale & Traditional 
Trading. Between 03/03/2026 and 06/26/2026, Sundex Trading Limited received 56 
wire transfers totaling $3,425,039.74, with individual transaction amounts ranging 
from $1,500.00 to $190,047.00. These wires were received from counterparties 
including Astoria Rx Holdings Inc, Happy Healthy Adult Daycare Inc, D and V Adult 
Day Care Center Inc, Allen Street Pharmacy Inc, Junhe Pharmacy LLC, We Care U Inc, 
Beautiful Palace Social AD Daycare, Borough Adult Day Care Inc, Asian Senior Day 
Care Corp, Sweet Home Adult Day Care Inc, Crystal Lake Senior Daycare Inc, Golden 
Elite Adult Day Care Inc, and Myrtle Wellness Pharmacy Corp. The majority of 
counterparties are identified as adult/senior day care operators or pharmacies, 
which is not consistent with the subject's stated industry type of Wholesale & 
Traditional Trading.

Multiple transactions were conducted on the same or near-consecutive days across 
the review period, including instances of multiple wires received from the same 
counterparty within a single week.

Transaction amounts included round-dollar and recurring values (e.g., $36,750.00, 
$42,000.00, $31,500.00) received on a recurring basis from the same counterparties 
throughout the period.

Open-source due diligence ("OSDD") identified a website for Sundex Trading Limited 
and a business registration record in Hong Kong. Per open sources, Sundex Trading 
Limited is registered as a Miscellaneous Durable Goods Merchant Wholesaler with a 
registered address at Room A5, 7/F, Astoria Building, 34 Ashley Road, Tsim Sha Tsui, 
Kowloon, Hong Kong. OSDD conducted on Astoria Rx Holdings Inc, Happy Healthy Adult 
Daycare Inc, and D and V Adult Day Care Center Inc identified these entities as 
registered businesses at the addresses on file; no information was identified 
establishing a business or economic relationship between these entities and Sundex 
Trading Limited. The source of funds and purpose of the transactions were not 
identified through OSDD.

No negative news was identified on Sundex Trading Limited.

An RFI was submitted to Airwallex to obtain additional information regarding 
Sundex Trading Limited and the activity. Airwallex responded that the client was 
suspended due to risk concerns and that Airwallex was unable to contact the exited 
client to obtain supporting documentation, as their procedures do not allow for 
further contact with clients following exit. No transaction support, invoices, or 
contracts were provided.

The activity is being escalated as it is inconsistent with the subject's stated 
industry type of Wholesale & Traditional Trading, given that counterparties are 
predominantly identified as adult/senior day care operators and pharmacies with 
no apparent business relationship to a wholesale trading company. The activity 
reflects a pattern of high-volume, high-frequency transactions totaling 
$3,425,039.74 across 56 transactions over a four-month period, for which the 
source of funds and purpose of the transactions could not be identified through 
OSDD. Additionally, Airwallex confirmed that the client was suspended due to risk 
concerns and was unable to provide supporting documentation in response to the RFI.
